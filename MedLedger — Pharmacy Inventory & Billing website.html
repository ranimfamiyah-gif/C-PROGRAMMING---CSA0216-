The challenge

Small tasks add up.

When records are handled by hand, everyday pharmacy work can become harder to track.

01Manual stock records take time to update and maintain.
02Low quantities can be difficult to spot before stock runs out.
03Expiry dates may be overlooked when checking records manually.
04Handwritten bills can slow service and lead to calculation errors.
System modules

One connected view of daily operations.

Project objectives

Designed to make the routine clearer.

Five practical goals guide the system concept.

01 / RECORDS

Store and manage medicine details in one place.

02 / STOCK

Update quantities when medicines are added or sold.

03 / ALERTS

Identify low-stock and near-expiry medicines.

04 / BILLING

Calculate prices and prepare bills accurately.

05 / EFFICIENCY

Reduce manual tasks in daily pharmacy operations.
